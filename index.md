# Dr Mitchell Buckley

**Proof Engineer and Mathematician** — Sydney, Australia

Mathematician and engineer. I work on complex problems and develop solutions that are both pragmatic and elegant. I love diving deep into intricate software systems to identify the core difficulties and resolving them to produce efficient and maintainable products.

## Work History

### Senior Proof Engineer — Trustworthy Systems (2018–present)

Major contributor to formal verification of the MCS (Mixed Criticality Systems) seL4 microkernel.

- Developed seL4 specifications written in Isabelle and Haskell to accommodate MCS features added to seL4 source (C).
- Identified subtle bugs in the logic of MCS and worked with systems engineers to develop robust fixes.
- Strengthened core lemmas to their strongest possible forms, improving maintainability of the proof base.
- Developed new proof infrastructure which streamlined the proofs and made them easier to read, write, and maintain.

Binary verification: ported the ARM binary verification tool for use on RISC-V.

- Found and fixed key failures in original research code (C, Python), as well as in external dependencies.
- Worked closely with the project leader to clarify requirements, goals, and timelines.

### Postdoctoral Fellow — Universit&eacute; Libre de Bruxelles (2015–2018)

Categorical Hopf algebra research with Joost Vercruysse. Developed the concept of *Oplax Hopf algebra* as a common generalisation of Hopf algebras and Hopf categories.

### Postdoctoral Researcher — CoACT, Macquarie University (2015)

Higher category theory. Built definitions of the orientals and cubes using a globular model of strict &omega;-categories.

### CSIRO Graduate Fellow — Data61 (2010–2011)

Image analysis. Implemented *Embedded Voxel Colouring*, a computer vision algorithm for generating 3D models from calibrated photographs (C++). Applied image segmentation research to mammogram analysis.

## Education

### PhD in Mathematics — Macquarie University (2011–2015)

Department of Computing and the Centre of Australian Category Theory (CoACT). Supervised by Professor Dominic Verity and Dr Richard Garner.

- Developed the first definition of *fibred bicategory* together with a full theory of fibrations for bicategories.
- Discovered the Catalan simplicial set: a combinatorial object which describes monoidal structures and their coherence data in arbitrarily large dimension.
- Formally verified Ross Street's theory of *parity complexes* in Coq: [github.com/MitchellBuckley/Parity-Complexes](https://github.com/MitchellBuckley/Parity-Complexes).

### BSc (First Class Honours) — Macquarie University (2005–2008)

GPA: 3.88/4. Advanced Mathematics program. Honours thesis on Lawvere theories.
